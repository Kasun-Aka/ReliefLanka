## 📌 Problem Statement

Sri Lanka experiences recurring seasonal flooding and disaster events that leave affected families needing fast access to emergency assistance. Relief organizations and volunteers often struggle to coordinate requests, donation drop-off points, field volunteers, and supply stock across scattered spreadsheets, phone calls, and messaging groups. During the critical first 72 hours, this lack of a shared, real-time system leads to duplicated effort, mismatched supply and demand (e.g., a district flooded with rice donations while medicine runs out), and inefficient volunteer deployment.

**ReliefLanka** is a centralized MERN-stack web platform that connects four core disaster-response functions into one system, organized by Sri Lankan districts so relief efforts can be matched directly to real-time local needs.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives & Scope

* **Emergency Requests:** Allow affected individuals to quickly submit and track relief requests.
* **Collection Directory:** Provide donors and the public with a searchable directory of active drop-off centers.
* **Volunteer Deployment:** Enable volunteers to register their skills and availability, helping coordinators deploy responders where needed.
* **Real-time Inventory Tracking:** Give relief coordinators visibility into available stock matched to specific districts.
* **District-centric Design:** Organize all modules around Sri Lanka's 25 administrative districts as the unifying geographic thread.
